What each stage does to the numbers

The retargeting pipeline as the tensors actually flow through it: a human hand arrives as 21 points in millimetres and leaves as 16 joint angles on a bus, and every stage between is either changing the shape or changing what the numbers mean. A · The shared vocabulary

Three objects are built once and read by everything downstream — the offline solve and the live loop use the same forward kinematics and the same collision proxy, which is the only reason a label and a live pose are comparable at all.

A0 · SENSOR

Two views of one hand

learning/sessions.py

The Leap reports a 5×5 bone array in device millimetres. It becomes two views of the same frame, because no single one survives both operations: per-digit scaling and the rest rotation preserve directions but destroy distances. A pinch that is 21 mm at the sensor measures 136 mm after scaling — the solver matched the 136 for a whole recording before this split existed.

bones (n, 5, 5, 3) mm → aligned (n, 21, 3) m raw (n, 21, 3) m

aligned — directions survive

$$x^{\text{al}}_i \;=\; R_{\text{robot}}\,R_{\text{rest}}\;\big(s_{d(i)}\,(x_i - x_{\text{wrist}})\big) \;+\; o_{d(i)}$$

raw — distances survive

$$x^{\text{raw}}_i \;=\; R_{\text{robot}}\,(x_i - x_{\text{wrist}}),\qquad \lVert x^{\text{raw}}_a - x^{\text{raw}}_b\rVert = \lVert x_a - x_b\rVert$$

A1 · DISTANCE TABLE

The hand as 15 scalars

learning/geometry.py

Every fingertip pair and every curl, in millimetres, off the raw view. The first three cells are the thumb row — thumb to index, middle, ring — and they become the intent signal that tells the objective which contact the operator is asking for.

raw (n, 21, 3) → cells (n, 15) mm intent (n, 3)

per-cell distance, and the intent ramp

$$d_c \;=\; \lVert x^{\text{raw}}_{a(c)} - x^{\text{raw}}_{b(c)} \rVert, \qquad c = 1 \ldots 15$$ $$\iota_j \;=\; \operatorname{clip}\!\Big(\tfrac{d_{\text{far}} - d_j}{d_{\text{far}} - d_{\text{near}}},\,0,\,1\Big),\quad d_{\text{far}} = 120\,\text{mm}$$

A2 · KINEMATICS + PROXY

Where the links are, and how close

learning/kinematics.py · learning/collision.py

One differentiable FK walk gives every body's origin and frame. The collision proxy wraps each of the 4 bodies per digit in an oriented box measured off its mesh, and reports a separating-axis gap for all 108 pairs — 96 digit-against-digit plus 12 against the palm. One offset per pair is fitted against MuJoCo's true mesh distance, so the proxy is a calibrated stand-in rather than a guess.

q (b, 16) rad → origins (b, 4, 5, 3) frames (b, 4, 5, 3, 3) gaps (b, 108) mm
bodies/digit 4 gap_version 2 agrees with MuJoCo 99.4%

separating-axis gap, minus the fitted offset

$$g_{p} \;=\; \max_{k \in \mathcal{A}}\Big(\,\lvert \Delta c \cdot a_k\rvert \;-\; r^A_k \;-\; r^B_k \Big)\;-\;\delta_p$$ $$r^A_k=\textstyle\sum_{j}\lvert a_k\!\cdot\! u^A_j\rvert\,h^A_j, \qquad \mathcal{A} = \{u^A_j\} \cup \{u^B_j\} \cup \{u^A_i \times u^B_j\}$$

the degenerate-axis guard — nine of the fifteen axes need it

$$k \in \mathcal{A} \iff \lVert u^A_i \times u^B_j \rVert^2 > 10^{-6}$$

B · Offline — making the labels

The teacher is an optimiser, not a network. It is allowed to be slow because it runs once; what it produces is the target the student spends its life imitating.

B1 · TEACHER SOLVE

600 Adam steps through the FK

learning/objective.py · learning/teacher.py

A whole run is solved at once, warm-started from the live NLopt solver so the trajectory begins somewhere plausible. The objective scores directions against the aligned view and contact against the raw one, and charges penetration on all 108 pairs.

aligned + raw + intent → q* (54953, 16) rad
steps 600 lr 0.02 cosine smooth savgol 21/2 tol 1 mm clearance 4 mm

what is minimised, per run

$$\mathcal{L} \;=\; \underbrace{\bar\theta_{\text{bone}}}_{\text{posture}} \;+\; \underbrace{w_c\,\overline{\tau\,g^{+}_{\text{tip}}}}_{\text{contact}} \;+\; \underbrace{w_t\,\overline{\lvert d^{r} - \sigma d^{h}\rvert}}_{\text{track}} \;+\; w_a\,\overline{\lvert \ddot q \rvert} \;+\; \Lambda_{\text{limit}}$$ $$\;+\;\underbrace{w_g\!\!\sum_{p=1}^{108}\!\big[\alpha_p - g_p\big]_{+}^{2}}_{\text{self-collision}} \;+\; w_o\,\big[\!-g_{\text{obj}}\big]_{+}^{2} \;+\; w_h\,\overline{\big[g_{\text{obj}}\big]_{+}}$$

term weights, as shipped

$$w_c\!=\!1.0,\;\; w_t\!=\!0.2,\;\; w_a\!=\!1.5,\;\; w_g\!=\!0.3,\;\; w_{\text{order}}\!=\!0.3,\;\; w_o\!=\!3.0,\;\; w_h\!=\!4.0$$

B2 · OBJECTS

The cylinder rides in, then is pinned

learning/gestures.py · learning/objective.py

An object solved against from a standing start stalls: closing a finger drives the tip through the surface before the middle link lies on it, and the penetration wall stops the finger there. So the object first rides with the fingers at its own learning rate, then slides back to where the operator's hand placed it while the fingers follow.

hand cylinder (c, a, r) → robot capsule p0, p1, r
ride 300 steps object lr 1 mm return 100 steps polish 100 @ lr/4

placed from the hand — one wrap, one label

$$c_{\text{robot}} \;=\; \Big(\,\underbrace{f + (r + \ell)}_{\text{on the palm face}},\;\; k_y + \sigma\,R\,\Delta_y,\;\; k_z + \big(a_0 + a_1 r\big)\Big)$$

the object's shift, phase one only

$$s^{(t+1)} = s^{(t)} - \eta_{o}\,\nabla_{\!s}\mathcal{L},\qquad \eta_o = 10^{-3}\,\text{m} \;\ll\; \eta_q$$

B3 · SYNTHETIC

100 hands nobody recorded

learning/synthetic.py

The recording covers what one operator did; the sampler covers the corners they never visited. Fitted from the real session — joint ranges, tendon coupling, spread bands — then deliberately oversampled where the gesture probe found gaps, and solved by the same teacher.

sampler → keypoints (75000, 21, 3) q* (75000, 16)
hands 100 × 6 s rate 125 Hz objects 180 in 84 hands pinch rate 0.30

tendon coupling — one curl per hand, with departure

$$\phi_d \;=\; \phi_{\text{hand}} + \epsilon_d,\qquad \text{pip} = 1.15\,\phi_d,\qquad \text{dip} = 0.66\,\text{pip}$$

coverage gate before any of it is written

$$\min_{m}\;\Pr\big[\,q^{\text{real}}_{5}(m) \le s_m \le q^{\text{real}}_{95}(m)\,\big] \;\ge\; 0.98$$

C · The student

One causal network replaces the 600-step solve. Everything about its shape is a constraint rather than a layer count — causal because it has to run live, a pose basis because the answer has to be a pose a hand could hold.

C1 · FEATURES

81 numbers per frame

learning/student.py

Both views go in, because the network needs what each one preserves. The derivative channels were tried and removed: a Jacobian attribution put 99% of the output's frame-to-frame motion on velocity, acceleration and jerk, and under 1% on everything else — on a hand holding still.

63 aligned 15 cells 3 intent → x (b, 20, 81)

the feature vector

$$x_t \;=\; \big[\;\operatorname{vec}(X^{\text{al}}_t)\;\|\;d_t / 100\;\|\;\iota_t\;\big] \;\in\; \mathbb{R}^{81}$$

C2 · CAUSAL TCN

Dilations 1–2–4–8, one-sided

learning/student.py

Four blocks of two kernel-3 convolutions, left-padded and trimmed so the output at frame t cannot see t+1. The normalisation is cumulative over the frames so far — GroupNorm pooled statistics over time, which made the earlier supervised frames acausal.

x (b, 20, 81) → h (b, 20, 96)
channels 96 receptive field 61 fr supervised last 8 dropout 0.1

receptive field — two convolutions per block

$$\mathrm{RF} \;=\; 1 + 2\!\cdot\!\underbrace{2}_{\text{convs}}\!\sum_{\ell} D_\ell \;=\; 1 + 4(1{+}2{+}4{+}8) \;=\; 61 \;>\; 20$$

cumulative norm — causal by construction

$$\hat h_t \;=\; \frac{h_t - \mu_{1:t}}{\sqrt{\sigma^2_{1:t} + \varepsilon}}, \qquad \mu_{1:t} = \tfrac{1}{t}\textstyle\sum_{s\le t} h_s$$

C3 · HEADS

A pose basis, and two it never commands

learning/student.py · learning/train.py

The pose head writes its answer in a PCA basis fitted on the teacher's own solutions, at the full 16 components — ten carried 98.3% of the variance and cost 1.4°. Four horizons come out of one trunk. The contact head predicts a pinch, measured: it reads 0.00 through an entire wrap on a cylinder.

h (b, 20, 96) → q (b, 20, 4, 16) contact (b, 20, 3) ttc (b, 20, 3)
horizons 0, 5, 10, 15 fr basis 16 params 238,422

decode — zero-initialised, so an untrained net emits the mean pose

$$\hat q_{t,\,h} \;=\; \bar q \;+\; W\,z_{t,h},\qquad W \in \mathbb{R}^{16\times16},\; z = \text{head}(h_t)$$

training objective

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{student}} = \lVert \hat q - q^{*}\rVert_1 + \lambda_{\!p}\lVert \Delta^{\text{tip}}\rVert^2_2 + \lambda_{\!v}\lVert \dot{\hat q} - \dot q^{*}\rVert^2_2 + \lambda_{\!g}\big[\alpha - g\big]^2_{+} + \lambda_{\!u}\big[g - g^{*}\big]^2_{+} + \lambda_{\!h}\mathcal{H}$$ $$\lambda_p\!=\!20,\;\; \lambda_v\!=\!3.0,\;\; \lambda_g\!=\!8\!\times\!10^{-3},\;\; \lambda_u\!=\!10^{-3},\;\; \lambda_h\!=\!0.2$$

D · Live — the 100 Hz loop

Everything below runs between one sensor frame and the next. The stages are in command order: nothing reaches a servo without passing all of them.

D1 · REPAIR

Gauss–Newton back out of itself

learning/collision.py · mimic.py

A regressor accurate to 7–13° interpolating two clear poses can pass through one. Six steps on the proxy move the answer the least it needs to clear; a frame already clear is returned untouched and costs nothing.

q (16,) → q clear (16,)
steps 6 clamp 0.1 rad clear frame <2 ms

one step, on the squared penetration only

$$f(q) = \tfrac12\textstyle\sum_p \big[\tau - g_p(q)\big]^2_{+},\qquad q \leftarrow q - \operatorname{clip}\!\Big(\tfrac{0.9\,\cdot\,2f}{\lVert \nabla f\rVert^2}\,\nabla f\Big)$$

D2 · GUARD

Mesh-exact, before the bus

robot/collide.py

The proxy is a stand-in; this is the real mesh check, bisecting along the path from the last safe pose to the requested one. A refused target is not an error — the hand travels as far as it can and stops.

q request → q safe blocked bool
pairs 160 margin 6 mm clear 1.6 ms

the largest admissible step along the path

$$q_{\text{safe}} \;=\; q_0 + \beta^{\star}(q_1 - q_0),\qquad \beta^{\star} = \max\{\beta : \text{free}(q_0 + \beta\Delta q)\}$$

D3 · EXECUTOR

Close until something stops you

executor.py

Copying an angle grabs air — the robot's links are 21 mm thick where a finger is 8. So with GRIP on, each digit's closing joints keep going until the command runs ahead of the encoder while the encoder is not moving. That is contact, with no contact sensor. A pinch is excluded: the contact head says which stalls are the hand meeting itself.

target + encoders + mA → held at θ* + 0.06
ahead 0.10 rad for 0.15 s still <0.3 rad/s or >180 mA

the stall predicate, per digit

$$\text{stall} \;=\; \Big(\big(\bar a > 0.10 \;\wedge\; \max_j\lvert\dot\theta_j\rvert < 0.3\big) \;\vee\; \max_j I_j > 180\Big) \;\wedge\; \neg\,\text{pinch}$$ $$\bar a \;=\; \operatorname{mean}_j\big(\underbrace{u_j}_{\text{commanded}} - \theta_j\big) \quad\text{— not the mapping's wish}$$

D4 · BUS

Slew, write, read back

hardware.py

One daemon thread owns the port. Writes go out at 100 Hz behind a software slew limit; encoders and currents come back at 20. The executor runs before the limiter, so a held finger is pinned before it is slewed and the stall is honoured at a rate the hand can obey.

q (16,) rad → ticks (16,) → 16 × XL330
write 100 Hz read 20 Hz slew 6 rad/s current 300 mA

rate limit, per tick

$$u^{(t+1)} = u^{(t)} + \operatorname{clip}\big(y - u^{(t)},\; \pm v_{\max}\,\Delta t\big),\qquad v_{\max} = 6\ \text{rad/s}$$

angle to tick, per joint, sign measured not assumed

$$\text{tick}_j \;=\; 2048 \;+\; s_j\,\frac{\theta_j}{2\pi}\,4096, \qquad s_j \in \{-1, +1\}$$
